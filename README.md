# Sales-Analysis-Power-BI-
1. For this sales analysis report I used several visualizations.
2. I used cards to indicate an estimate analysis of amount, quantity and profit.
3. Maps were used to represent profits by state.
4. Used donut chart and pie chart to show profit by category and profit by city respectively.
5. Used line chart to show Order by year and category.
6. The Key insights that I observed are-
7. Electronics, particularly Electronic Games and Laptops, are the highest revenue generators.
8. Credit Card is the most preferred payment method.
9. California leads in sales, followed by New York and Florida.
10. December is the peak sales month, likely due to holiday shopping.
